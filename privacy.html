Privacy Policy

Torchbound, by Nguyen Phuong. Last updated October 27, 2026.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics of our own. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Ads

Torchbound shows optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners (such as Unity Ads and ironSource Ads). To show and measure ads, these partners may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Before any ad, iOS asks whether you allow tracking; you can refuse, and you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Torchbound is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can refuse tracking, reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings, or delete the app to remove all game data on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
