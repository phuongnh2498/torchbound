Contact

Questions, bugs or purchase problems: email phuong@bootlab.co. We usually reply within two days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play? Drag anywhere on the screen to move. Your hero attacks on their own. Pick an upgrade each time you level up and survive the waves.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

I bought something and did not get it. Open the Store and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, email us with the date of the purchase.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Ads are always optional and only give extra rewards.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
